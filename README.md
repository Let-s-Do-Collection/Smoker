The Smoker – an often overlooked, rarely used, yet versatile block. This mod not only gives it new functions but also a whole new purpose.

<br>

Smoker is a fun addition to your vanilla experience and serves to give the Smoker a real reason to exist, without breaking vanilla immersion.

<br>

## Fire It Up

<br>

- The Improved Smoker: a reworked version of the vanilla block with genuine new mechanics
- Feed it different types of wood to change how it smokes
- Each wood grants its own modifiers, changing saturation and nutrition of your food
- Crafting time varies by material, so choose your fuel with care

<br>

Not all smoke is created equal.

<br>

## Smoke, Don't Just Cook

<br>

- Data-driven Smoker Modifier recipes, easy to extend and balance
- A dedicated interface showing your smoking progress at a glance
- Smooth, properly ticking smoking logic, no more stuck or broken smokers

<br>

Cook, smoke, and discover new possibilities.
